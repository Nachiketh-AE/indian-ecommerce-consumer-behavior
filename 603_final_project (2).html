Research Questions and Background

E-commerce platforms allow consumers to access a multitude of products in just a few seconds. Given the vast number of apps available today, we explore the following research questions in the context of the Indian market:

RQ1: What factors influence customer satisfaction when using e-commerce delivery apps?

Hypothesis: - Examine the relationship between delivery time, order value, and service rating/customer feedback.

- Expectation: An inverse relationship between delivery time and service rating; a direct relationship between order value and service rating.

RQ2: Are there patterns to order delays? Can we predict if an order may be delayed based on the type of product or platform?

Hypothesis: - Delays might be more frequent on specific platforms, time slots, or product categories.
